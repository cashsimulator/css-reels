# Asset library (owner-supplied, Nov 2026)

New characters and settings for upcoming videos. Not wired into `engine/scenes.py` yet: when a video uses one, add it there (a talking face needs the mouth position; b-roll needs a small motion definition).

## Characters
- **The Exec** (`exec_*`): silver-haired man, navy pinstripe suit, red tie, corner office with a city sunset. Two moods from the same shoot:
  - `exec_smile_*` (relaxed, glasses in some shots)
  - `exec_serious_*` (cold, focused)
  - shots: `desk_talking`, `reading_paper`, `hallway_tablet`, `window_back`, `computer`
  - Good for: the confident boss, "the board", the CEO who thinks numbers are fine, a rival to the CFO.
  - These are crops of a 5-panel collage, so each is low resolution (about 400 px). Use them as quick cutaways or pixel-art style shots, not full-screen talking close-ups.

## Settings
- `board_dinner_pov.jpg`: business dinner, POV from the table. Bald man in an olive jacket arguing, the Exec answering, a young waiter with a phone behind. Has burned-in text "POV: Trying to explain the Q4 margins to the board." at the bottom; crop or cover it if the hook changes.
- `wall_street_night.jpg` (16:9): Wall Street at night with the bull statue, PS2 style. Needs a 9:16 crop (the bull is centred).
- `trading_floor_office.jpg` (16:9): trading floor with screens. A small "NYSE" sign is visible in the middle; avoid framing it large.

## Not used, on purpose
- The portrait of a real, famous investor: real people's likeness in an ad implies an endorsement they never gave. Do not use it.
- The NYSE floor image full of Citadel, ICE and NYSE logos: real company brands in our ads are a trademark risk. Do not use it.
