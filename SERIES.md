# Series bible: "I ran the scenario"

Learned from the Unemployed Corp. teardown (Nov 2026): sell an identity through a recurring hero, a recurring foil, one question and one deadpan answer that carries the brand, a reveal that shows the product working, and the same end card every time. We copy the engine, never their characters, lines or look.

## Cast
- **Hero, Lina "the calm one"**: owns a small cleaning company. Teal polo, black braid, clipboard. Never rushes, never brags. She is the person the viewer wants to be: the owner who already knows her February.
- **Foil, Rick "best year ever"**: flashy owner (the coffee-shop owner or the Exec can play him). Always on the phone, always "crushing it", always surprised by his bank account.
- **The CFO**: stays the narrator of the explainer format and can cameo in skits (the one who says "told you").
- Supporting: the baker, Maya (first hire), the family at the table, the board.

## The question and the answer (every skit)
- The question sharpens each episode: "How are you so calm?" -> "How did you know?" -> "Who told you about February?"
- The answer, deadpan, always the same: **"I ran the scenario."** It is the product name said out loud.

## Motifs (what people recognise in one frame)
- **The HUD**: two game bars on screen, `PROFIT ████████` and `CASH ██░░░░`. Profit stays full while cash drains. Plus a date stamp ("FEB 26").
- **The red week**: the app's real "below zero Feb 26" screen is always the reveal.
- **Mantra**: "Know your February."
- **End card**: our DM BETA card, same every time.

## CTA ladder (only true claims)
1. "Private beta. DM me BETA for a code." (now)
2. "Beta spots are limited" only once there is a real cap; say the real number.
3. "Beta closes <date>" only if it really closes.
Never fake scarcity, fake sales or fake testimonials. If the caption promises something, the DM reply must deliver it the same day.

## Episode list (rotate the arena: work, family, friends, money, holidays)
1. **Profit: MAX. Cash: 0.** Rick lands three big contracts (rule of three, HUD: CONTRACT 1/3, 2/3, 3/3). Profit bar fills, cash bar drains each time. Lina next door is calm. "How are you so calm?" "I ran the scenario." Reveal: the app's red week on Rick's numbers.
2. **Same sales. Same city. Different February.** Split screen, matching dates. Lina (30-day terms) vs Rick (60-day terms). Rick's half turns red in week 8. Uses the real app screens: 60 days -14,000 / 30 days +33,000.
3. **Thanksgiving (post Nov 25).** Family dinner, Rick brags, Lina: "I ran the scenario." Reversal: "they told me you'd call me in February, Rick." Kid: "Mom, I want to run scenarios."
4. **Boss fight: The Late Payer.** Game boss fight; each "payment next week" takes HP off the cash bar. Power-up: the simulator.
5. **The board dinner.** "POV: trying to explain the Q4 margins to the board" (assets/library). The Exec is the foil; the CFO answers with the app.
6. **The conversion.** Rick finally asks: "Okay. Send me the code." End card shows both on the same side. This is the move we want from the viewer.

## Craft rules from the teardown
- Hook on screen in frame one (a HUD or a POV line). Payoff by second 25. 25 to 35 s.
- Lines of 6 words or fewer. One colour per speaker in the subtitles.
- 85 to 90 % story, offer only in the last 2 to 4 s.
- One moment of near-silence before the biggest gag.
- The product (the app screen) legible at least once, timed with the brand line.
- Track per episode: 3-second hold, completion, shares, comments quoting "I ran the scenario", DMs saying BETA.
